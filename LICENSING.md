# Licensing and provenance

The firmware is GPL-3.0-only (`LICENSE`). Distribution requires corresponding source under that license. The project is independent of SOMA Laboratory and M-VAVE.

## Independent audio engine

`firmware/engine/{p23_engine.c,p23_engine.h,p23_math.h}` is new GPL-3.0-only code written for this project. It uses published functional descriptions of the Pulsar-23 and independently implemented arithmetic, synthesis, graph state, filters, envelopes, mixing and effects. It contains no Bigorna audio implementation, DSP tables, voice allocator, post-synth chain, or effect tank. No SOMA firmware, samples, schematics or proprietary DCO/FX code have been copied.

## Retained control and platform code

The UI, sequencer, MIDI, phrase/event logic, session/pattern/loop storage, backup protocol, display, boot and FM-1 support derive from the recorded Bigorna build 80 source snapshot. They retain GPL-3.0-only attribution to Bigorna/Tonitor (Nuno Zimas), Felucca (Copyright (C) 2026 Leo Kuroshita, Hügelton Instruments), SLOOP (isod89), and Melodee (Copyright (C) 2026 Kerem Kilic, Ellic Studio) in their respective files.

Melodee-derived platform files include the dual-core start/hold/fault/mailbox infrastructure, supply setup and TRS MIDI parser. That platform infrastructure does not provide this project's audio engine. General frontend arithmetic and control LFO tables remain in the control layer and are not imported by the independent engine.

Terminus, Atkinson Hyperlegible and Michroma font assets retain their supplied SIL OFL licenses under `assets/fonts/`. JieLi AC79 SDK boot/config files are read from the installed SDK during packaging; the SDK is Apache-2.0 and is not part of this source tree.

The English SOMA manual and Synthdawg notebook under `docs/reference/` remain copyrighted by their authors. They are local research copies excluded from version control; this project's GPL grant does not cover those books.

The superseded hybrid build 1 is archived locally under the ignored `build/archive/` for recovery only; none of it is
published or used.

and `CHANGELOG.md`. Its pages, `docs/css/site.css`, `docs/js/menu.js`, `docs/js/manual.js`, `docs/js/demo.js`, the

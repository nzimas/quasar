Vascular — Tactile Synth

iPhone · iPad · Android phones and tablets

Canvas-based electroacoustic instrument. Touch canvas to create sound generators and processors, and connect them with your fingers: network is signal path, picture of instrument and surface it is played on. Node's position on canvas is its place in stereo field. Every change is heard at once.

Main features

  • 55 synthesis families: subtractive, FM, additive, granular, spectral, physical modelling, wavetable, wave terrain, pulsar and stochastic. Also DX7, 3 macrosynths, drum synthesis, 303 and supersaw.
  • 71 processors: filters, distortion, delays, combs, diffusion, reverb, spectral freezing, convolution, vocoding, tape loops and others.
  • Sample player for your own loops, sampled acoustic instruments that play in session's key, and microphone input.
  • 7 controllers that play or move other nodes: melodic sequencer, arpeggiator, drum machine, shift register, colliding cars, Game of Life and LFO controller.
  • Randomizer for whole patches or parts of them, with padlock on every parameter; Angiogenesis, which changes network on its own.
  • 27 scales and Scala tunings, shared clock with tap tempo, and per-source clock divisions.
  • 32 pattern slots with morphing, 4-lane effects sequencer, gesture looper, 48 levels of undo, and recording of takes.
  • Runs as AUv3 instrument in AUM, Loopy Pro, Logic and other hosts, and hosts AUv3 plugins as nodes (iOS, in-app purchase).

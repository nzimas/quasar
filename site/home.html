M-VAVE FM-1 · after the SOMA Pulsar-23 · new audio engine

Percussion synthesizer firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

Quasar replaces the FM-1's firmware. It models the SOMA Pulsar-23 — BD, BASS, SD and HHT, the SHAOS shift register, the LFO, the clock dividers and the FX processor — with an audio engine written from scratch. 6 tracks, a 16-step sequencer and a phrase generator, aimed at experimental IDM.

Demo phrases

6 tracks, phrases from the generator, STUTTER on the last beat of every 4th bar. Rendered by the firmware's code on a computer, not recorded from the device.

  1. Fracture — BD, WALL, STATIC, GRIND, FM, METAL 143 BPM
  2. Polymeter — HAMMER, TONE, SHAKE, BASS, PLUCK, HHT 127 BPM
  3. Overload — SKIN, SD, METAL, PRC, GRIND, WIRE 165 BPM

Features

New engine
Written from scratch for this firmware: integer arithmetic on the FM-1's 2 cores, no code or tables from other synthesizers.
16 engine profiles
BD, BASS, SD and HHT, plus HAMMER, SKIN, WALL, WIRE, TONE, STATIC, METAL, SHAKE, GRIND, FM, PRC and PLUCK. Each of the 6 tracks plays any of them.
147 sound families
HOME draws a random sound from the profile's families: kicks, snares, metal, static, grinders, gongs, strings. Short decays.
Macros and routes
4 macros on the main knobs. 4 internal routes per sound, from SHAOS, the LFO, the clock dividers, or another track's output or envelope.
WRECK
Wavefolder, ring modulator, sample-rate and bit reduction after every voice.
Phrase generator
Polymeters, per-track clock and direction, Euclidean layers, ratchets, off-grid hits. Parameter locks reach WRECK.
BREAK
10 ways to break the pattern while a key is held: REPEAT, HALF TIME, DOUBLE, REVERSE, CHOP, ROLL, SHUFFLE, POLY, JUMP, SKIP. Random layers add ratchets up to 8, rises, WRECK and STUTTER.
STUTTER
Beat repeat of the whole output on its own key. SLICE, RATE, MODE and DECAY can each be set to RND.
PERFORMANCE
Change anything, then press the key again to restore the previous state. CTRL ALL applies changes to every track with notes. Save a pattern to keep the result.
Sequencer
16 steps, 8 parameter locks per step, trig conditions, MUTATE, MIDI FX, patterns, sessions, MIDI and MIDI LEARN. The control layer comes from Bigorna, the same author's FM-1 firmware.

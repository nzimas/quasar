M-VAVE FM-1 · after the SOMA Pulsar-23 · engine written from scratch

A firmware for experimental IDM, built around raw energy

Quasar turns the FM-1 into a percussion synthesizer modelled on the SOMA Pulsar-23: its kick, bass, snare and hat circuits, its SHAOS shift register, LFO, clock dividers and FX processor, rewritten from nothing for the FM-1's chip. It is made for broken, fractured, polymetric rhythm: Autechre more than anything else, rhythmic noise as an afterthought. Every sound is percussive and every sound is hard.

A quasar is among the brightest things in the universe: a galaxy's core, where a black hole throws out jets of matter at nearly the speed of light and outshines every star around it. That is the idea here — not the calm of deep space, but a point of energy released all at once.

Features

A new engine
Written from scratch for this firmware: integer arithmetic on the FM-1's two cores, no tables or code from any other synthesizer. What it models, and where it had to give way.
16 engine profiles
The Pulsar-23's four circuits, BD, BASS, SD and HHT, each in several modes: HAMMER, SKIN, WALL, WIRE, TONE, STATIC, METAL, SHAKE, GRIND, FM, PRC, PLUCK. Six tracks; each plays any of them.
147 families
HOME draws a sound from the profile's families: kicks, snares, metal, static, grinders, gongs, strings. Each drawing is different. No polite sounds, no drones, no long decays.
Macros and routes
Four macros on the main knobs, four internal routes per sound from SHAOS, the LFO, the clock dividers or another track's output and envelope.
WRECK
A wavefolder, a ring modulator, sample-rate and bit reduction after every voice.
Phrase generator
Polymeters, tracks on their own clocks and directions, crossed Euclidean layers, ratchets, hits pulled off the grid. Locks reach WRECK step by step.
BREAK
Ten ways of breaking a pattern while a key is held, after breakcore and Autechre — shuffled, polymetric, jumping, skipping — with ratchets to 8, rises, wreck and stutter layers. Nothing ever drops out.
STUTTER
A beat repeat of the whole output on its own key, with RND settings so each press is different.
PERFORMANCE
A key that keeps the state: change anything, then let it all go back. CTRL ALL moves every track with notes at once. Keep what you like as a pattern.
From the step sequencer up
16 steps with eight parameter locks a step, trig conditions, MUTATE and MIDI FX, patterns, sessions, MIDI and MIDI LEARN — the control layer of Bigorna, the same author's FM-1 firmware.
